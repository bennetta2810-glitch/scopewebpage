How it works

From click to answer in a few seconds.

  1. Start a selectionClick the Scope AI icon or press Alt+Shift+S. The page dims and your cursor becomes a crosshair.
  2. Drag a boxCover the question, paragraph, or figure you want help with. Letting go captures just that area.
  3. Capture and cropThe extension takes a screenshot of the visible tab and crops it to your box, right in your browser.
  4. Ask the AIThe cropped image is sent to the Scope AI model using your access code.
  5. Read the answerA panel appears in the corner with the explanation. Ask follow-ups or close it with ×.

Under the hood

Manifest V3

Built on Chrome’s current extension platform, with minimal permissions.

activeTab only

It can only see a tab when you trigger it, never in the background.

Shadow DOM panel

The answer UI is isolated, so it won’t break or be broken by the site you’re on.

Scope Browser

A desktop web browser with Scope AI built in. Select anything on any page and get an instant explanation, without installing an extension or switching tabs.

What Scope Browser does

  • Select and explain: drag a box over any text, equation, chart, diagram, or code on a webpage, and Scope AI explains it in a side panel.
  • Ask follow-ups: keep asking questions about what you selected. Scope remembers the context.
  • Works on anything you can see: PDFs in the browser, scanned handouts, images with text, and online textbooks.
  • Nothing extra to install: Scope Lens is built in, so there is no Chrome extension or developer-mode setup.
  • Linked to your account: sign in with your Scope AI account to use your Max access.
